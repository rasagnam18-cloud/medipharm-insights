Frequently asked questions

What clients typically ask.

What does pharmaceutical competitive intelligence cover?

Competitive intelligence can cover clinical trials, pipeline assets, regulatory milestones, competitor strategy, launches, partnerships and market developments.

Can you support market research for a specific therapy area?

Yes. Research can be structured around therapy areas such as oncology, immunology, metabolic conditions and rare disease, depending on the business question.

What types of analytics can be delivered?

Typical outputs include executive dashboards, trend analysis, monitoring frameworks, evidence synthesis and forecasting-oriented analytical views.

How do I start a project?

Share the decision, therapy area, geography, timeframe and evidence challenge through our contact page. We can then scope the appropriate research approach.
